ABOUT THE OFFICE

Student health, built around students.

The Office of the Medical Director works to promote health education, preventive care, student wellbeing and meaningful health advocacy throughout the OOU student community.

This platform is designed to serve students across Ago-Iwoye, Sagamu, Aiyetoro and Ibogun, and to remain useful beyond the tenure of any one officeholder.
